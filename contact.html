Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Helpful answers regarding reservations, check-in, policies, and bespoke hotel amenities.

What are the hotel check-in and check-out times?

Standard check-in time is 2:00 PM and check-out is 12:00 PM. Early check-in and late check-out can be facilitated based on suite availability or arranged in advance via our concierge.

How does offline telephone / WhatsApp booking work?

You can call our 24/7 dedicated reservation desk at +91 123 456 7890 or ping our WhatsApp concierge. Our managers will hold your preferred suite, offer special corporate/member tariffs, and send a secured payment link or invoice for direct bank settlement.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

Reservations may be cancelled free of penalty up to 24 hours prior to 2:00 PM on the scheduled arrival date. Peak holiday bookings or presidential suites may have special festive deposit terms outlined during reservation.

Do you arrange private airport chauffeurs?

Yes, our fleet includes luxury Mercedes-Benz S-Class sedans and Rolls-Royce motorcars. We provide tarmac/exit lounge VIP meet-and-assist directly from Indira Gandhi International Airport.
